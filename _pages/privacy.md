## Hosting

This website is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com), a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, the requested page, date and time, and browser information to GitHub's servers. GitHub needs this data to deliver the website and to keep its service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Visitor statistics

To understand how this website is used, I use [Umami](https://umami.is), a privately operated analytics installation at `umami.tony-luke.de`, hosted in Germany. Umami does not set tracking cookies or track visitors across different websites.

It processes the visited page and its URL parameters (including campaign tags), the referring website, browser and operating system, device and screen size, language, and approximate location. The IP address is processed to derive the location and calculate a hashed session identifier; it is not stored as an IP-address field in the analytics records. This identifier groups visits without cookies, but does not guarantee complete anonymity. The purpose is to understand page usage and improve this website; the legal basis for these visitor statistics is my legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Technical details are described in the [Umami documentation](https://docs.umami.is/docs/metric-definitions).

## Cookies and local storage

This website does not use cookies. If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so that it is kept on your next visit. This data never leaves your device.

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, contact me at the email address above.
