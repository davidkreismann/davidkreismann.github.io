This page explains which data is processed when you visit this website.

## Hosting

This website is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com), a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, the requested page, date and time, and browser information to GitHub's servers. GitHub needs this data to deliver the website and to keep its service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Visitor statistics

To see how often pages are visited, this website uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com). GoatCounter does not set cookies and does not track you across websites. It records the visited page, the referring website, browser and operating system, screen size and the country derived from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored, and the statistics cannot be linked to you as a person. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in the [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

## Cookies and local storage

This website does not use cookies. If you switch between light and dark mode or between English and German, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so that it is kept on your next visit. This data never leaves your device.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, contact me at the email address above.
