I am a Master's student in **Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence** at the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de) and a working student at [IBM](https://www.ibm.com).

Along the way I have worked on research projects at the CV4DT Lab at the University of Cambridge, where I am currently working on using generative AI to design soft robotic grippers. Before that I was at IBM Research in Zurich and Almaden and at the Karlsruhe Service Research Institute.

* Deep Learning
